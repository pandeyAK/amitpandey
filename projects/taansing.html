The Challenge

Generic advice, and nothing that makes people come back

As a personal side project, the goal was twofold: test how far AI-assisted tooling could carry a small team through genuinely end-to-end product development — research, design, and engineering — and solve a real gap in the singing-app market, where most apps give generic advice instead of an objective read on a user's actual voice, and struggle to keep users coming back long enough to improve.

The Solution

An objective voice diagnosis, built almost entirely with AI

I founded Taansing (initially as a hobby project), built almost entirely with AI-assisted workflows across the stack — from product research and UX/brand design to Android-native engineering in Kotlin/Jetpack. At the core, the solution is a GRBAS-based voice analysis engine grounded in published clinical acoustic parameters (Jitter, Shimmer, HNR) that gives users an objective diagnosis of their voice and recommends songs matched to it, paired with gamified retention mechanics (pitch highway, Daily Voice Check, Challenge Mode) to tackle the habit-formation problem.

The AI-native build stack: Cursor and Claude Code connected via MCP into Replit, backed by a dedicated context library and a governance/eval layer to keep AI-generated work reliable across the stack.
The Impact

0 to 1, bootstrapped

I took the consumer-facing product from 0 to 1, from concept to a fully branded, working app, end to end: product strategy, UX, technical partnerships, and go-to-market, while deliberately bootstrapping rather than raising early.

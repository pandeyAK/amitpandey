The Challenge

Fragmented liquidity, manual operations, real counterparty risk

Crypto market liquidity was fragmented across dozens of exchanges, forcing institutional and professional trading teams to manage assets manually across separate exchange accounts. This created operational overhead and left funds exposed to counterparty risk while sitting on exchange wallets outside self-custody with limited governance over who could authorize withdrawals across a growing team.

The Solution

One command center, self-custody preserved

I led Product for Exchange Hub, a unified dashboard (with API capability) built into Qredo's MPC-secured self-custody platform. It lets clients control assets across major exchanges (Binance, OKX, Huobi, ByBit) from a single command center, move funds in real time via exchange APIs, and settle back to a secure self-custody Vault in seconds — with configurable governance controls scoping withdrawal rights to designated workspace members, from small teams to hundreds of users. The complete solution was built in 3 months using a tight feedback loop with design partners.

The Impact

$390M in AUC within the first month

We launched with 4 major CEX integrations, with 6 more in the pipeline. AUC grew to $390 million in the first month. Each Exchange Hub transaction paid 10-50 basis points as revenue. As the assets stayed in self-custody, platform trust increased.
